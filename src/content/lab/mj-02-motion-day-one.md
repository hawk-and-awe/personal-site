When Midjourney released video, I spent four days running 270+ of my favorite archive stills through it, testing low vs. high motion on everything from painted fantasy to fake VHS to product shots.

The goal was to learn what motion the model understands — camera drift, hair and cloth, liquids, crowds — and where it breaks: faces under heavy motion, text, hands. That way I know when to reach for it in real work.
