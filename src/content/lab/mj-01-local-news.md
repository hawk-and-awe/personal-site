A study in broadcast-format emulation. A six-word prompt tail reliably produces the whole look: interlaced softness, news-van color, the handheld framing of a local crew. A knight jousting a clothesline in a suburban backyard; something in the drainage canal; an apparition in a middle-school gym.

Most frames were then animated, which is where it really works. It reads as found footage.
