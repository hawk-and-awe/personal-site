We traveled to three dealerships in the Classic family to film this case study. Bently, chief operating officer of Classic Chevrolet and the group's general counsel, explains how one-on-one benefits enrollment reaches nearly every employee, even with one or two payroll people in most stores, and helps him keep the people he has trained.

In 2025 I planned the trip and the schedule, worked the on-location multi-cam shoot and its interviews, then cut and color-graded the film. I also built a reusable, branded After Effects case-study template, and the finished film runs through it: the title card, the duotone “Before” section, pull quotes set beside the interview and animated figures for participation and take-up.
