Video Projects

Sales Conference: Infographic video

As part of a full conference video experience for our company’s Sales Conference in 2019, I was tasked with building out an impressive data-driven infographic video from scratch. It was displayed on a massive 100’ wide video display to the entire sales organization.

Enrollment Platform Promo

As we launched a major update to our customer enrollment platform, this ad was to show how our comprehensive interactive enrollment platform set us apart from our competitors.

 

Guided Enrollment | Pandemic Safety

During COVID-19 in 2020, many of our account managers adjusted how enrollments were done. This video was to reassure customers that safety was our priority. The clip above showcases the sections of the video project that I specifically animated.

Billing platform promo

This video showcases one of the biggest features offered in our company’s billing platform. The hands-free option was a major time-saver for our employer customers and helped take some of the burden off their shoulders

 

Indiana Customer Testimonial

In 2019 we traveled to four cities in Indiana over the course of a week and filmed numerous customer testimonials. I took part in the planning, logistics for travel, as well as aiding in full production and post-production roles for the entire project. This video was one of six that we created as a result of the production.

Danny Customer Testimonial

In early 2020 we traveled to Fresno, CA to film this story from an employee customer who used our Cancer benefits to help him through the most challenging time of his life. I took part in the planning, logistics for travel, as well as aiding in the full production and post-production roles for the entire project.

Billing new feature promo

In 2021 we began rolling out a major new feature to our billing platform. This video promo was to inform our employer customers about the big changes and how it will save them time month after month. I was involved in the pre-production, production on set and handled the color and animation for the entire project.
